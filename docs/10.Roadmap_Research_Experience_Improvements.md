# Roadmap: Research Experience Improvements

## Overview

**Epic:** Research Experience Improvements

**Status:** SELESAI (semua fase diimplementasikan dan di-merge)

### Goal

Enhance the existing AI research experience by improving workflow
continuity, response quality, and overall usability without rebuilding
capabilities that already exist.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 1 --- Workflow Consistency

**Status: SELESAI** (PR #161, #163)

## Objective

Make the research workflow feel seamless and consistent across all
supported user interactions.

### Scope

-   Improve workflow continuity
-   Eliminate inconsistent behavior
-   Provide a more predictable research experience

### Expected Outcome

Users experience a consistent research workflow regardless of how
research is initiated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 2 --- Conversation Continuity

**Status: SELESAI** (PR #165)

## Objective

Enable research conversations to build naturally upon previous
interactions.

### Scope

-   Preserve meaningful conversation context
-   Reuse previous research when appropriate
-   Improve long-term continuity

### Expected Outcome

Research sessions become more natural, connected, and context-aware.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 3 --- Research Quality

**Status: SELESAI** (PR #169)

## Objective

Continuously improve the quality and usefulness of AI-generated
research.

### Scope

-   Improve clarity
-   Improve consistency
-   Improve reasoning
-   Improve overall user experience

### Expected Outcome

Research becomes easier to understand and more valuable for investment
decision support.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recommended Implementation Order

1.  Workflow Consistency — selesai (PR #161, #163)
2.  Conversation Continuity — selesai (PR #165)
3.  Research Quality — selesai (PR #169)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Criteria

-   Research workflows behave consistently.
-   Previous research improves future interactions.
-   AI responses become clearer and more consistent.
-   User experience improves without increasing unnecessary complexity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Design Principles

-   Improve existing capabilities before introducing new ones.
-   Focus on user value rather than feature count.
-   Avoid duplicate functionality.
-   Keep improvements implementation-agnostic and outcome-focused.
-   Apply the simplest solution that satisfies current product needs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Out of Scope

This roadmap does **not** introduce new research capabilities or replace
the existing research engine. It focuses exclusively on improving the
quality, continuity, and usability of the current experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Alignment with Product Vision

This roadmap complements the existing AI research capability and remains
aligned with the project's PRD, architecture, CLI-first approach,
engine/interface separation, deterministic tooling, and
provider-agnostic design. It intentionally avoids prescribing
implementation details so it can be executed by AI coding agents while
minimizing over-engineering.
